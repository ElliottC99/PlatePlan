Codebase Context

  • Abstract recipes: RecipeFirestoreRepository.saveRecipe runs stripProductIdsFromIngredients, so recipes bind to subtypeId, ingredientId, or categoryId rather than concrete products.
  • Greedy single-line resolution: PantryTaxonomyService.resolveDynamicProductForIngredient resolves one line at a time via linear .filter() scans and isAutoDefault / compareProductsByStrategy (default highest_protein_per_kcal), ignoring the rest of the recipe and often pushing total calories into a deficit penalty.
  • Static macro masking: macroScoringUtils.extractRecipeMacros returns saved recipe.macros whenever > 0, hiding pantry updates from Vault Fit Scores and Planner selection.
  • Asymmetric dual-person curves: fitScoreCalculator.calculateMealFitScore scores per-serving (cal, prot) for Elliott and Chloe across wide 100-point plateaus (85–105% kcal, 100–120% protein).
  • Plan locking & shopping ticks: MealPlanGeneratorService.generateMealPlan currently skips freezeProductSelectionsForPlan, while ShoppingCalculationService already reads slot-scoped plan.productSelections ahead of dynamic resolution.

1. Join-Ordered Interval Branch-and-Bound with O(1) Vector Scoring

Search & Scoring Algorithm Confidence: 95% Complexity: Medium

Precompute each candidate product's per-serving (cal, prot) once via NutritionService.calculateItemNutrition, compile Elliott/Chloe meal targets into a zero-allocation scalar scoreMacroPair(cal, prot, ctx), sort variable lines by macro swing descending, and run Branch-and-Bound using exact suffix [remMin, remMax] interval bounds against the unimodal piecewise curves in calculateCalorieScore / calculateProteinScore. Parameterize by (variant, mealType, activeProfile) and fall back to 2D bucketed DP if a recipe exceeds 6 variable lines.

Basis
direct: src/utils/fitScoreCalculator.js:109-241, src/utils/macroScoringUtils.js:229-255, src/components/ProductSwapModal.js:289-315; external: interval-bounded Branch-and-Bound & query-planner join ordering.
Rationale
Guarantees the exact global Fit Score optimum in <2 ms per recipe on mobile without brute-force explosion or object cloning.
Downsides
Requires maintaining parity between scoreMacroPair and calculateSingleMealTypeFitScore if curve weights change.

2. Pre-Indexed Pantry Pools, Constant Folding & Macro-Equivalence Bucketing

Candidate Space & Culinary Constraints Confidence: 95% Complexity: Medium

Replace repeated O(N*M) linear .filter() scans in PantryTaxonomyService.resolveDynamicProductForIngredient with a single CatalogIndex (Maps by subtypeId, ingredientId, and category). Fold excludeNutrition, single-SKU, and low-variance lines (<5 kcal, <0.5 g protein spread) into a static (baseCal, baseProt) offset, and collapse macro-identical supermarket SKUs (±3 kcal, ±0.3 g protein) to one strategy/price representative before search.

Basis
direct: src/services/PantryTaxonomyService.js:676-753, src/services/NutritionService.js:168-251; external: compiler constant folding & Pareto dominance pruning.
Rationale
Shrinks typical 12-ingredient recipes down to 2–5 genuinely distinct variable lines so combinatorial search runs instantaneously even as Pantry grows.
Downsides
Small quantization buckets (±3 kcal) can theoretically shift a boundary score by 1 point unless bucket size stays tight.

3. Epoch-Memoized Dynamic Macros as Live Authority

Evaluation Triggers, Caching & UX Surfaces Confidence: 90% Complexity: Medium

Stop trusting stale static recipe.macros > 0 in extractRecipeMacros when pantry products are hydrated. Instead, maintain monotonic pantryEpoch and prefsEpoch counters in Store plus a reverse dependency map (ingredientId/subtypeId -> recipeIds), memoize solved (selections, macros, fitScore) per epoch in memory, pre-warm in idle slices on startup, and eliminate the duplicate getSortedRecipes → VaultRecipeCard re-scoring with zero startup Firestore recipe writes.

Basis
direct: src/utils/macroScoringUtils.js:104-199, src/views/RecipeVaultView.js:142, src/components/vault/VaultRecipeCard.js:334, src/services/CacheMaintenanceService.js:54-66; external: Salsa incremental memoization.
Rationale
Ends header-vs-ingredient macro drift and keeps Vault filtering/scrolling at 60 fps on phones without burning Firestore write quota.
Downsides
Recipes whose ingredients fail to resolve to any pantry product must still fall back to stored recipe.macros.

5. Binding-Granularity Guardrails & Bi-Directional Culinary Pools

Candidate Space & Culinary Constraints Confidence: 85% Complexity: Medium

Bypass defaultProductId/isAutoDefault hard locks during combo search and allow ingredientId-bound lines (plus explicitly unlocked siblings) to explore both leaner and fuller-fat products—curing the Calorie Deficit Paradox where lean defaults starve calories. Guard candidate pools with classifyIngredientRole and resolveProductItemWeight > 0 for discrete qty lines instead of the 6 hardcoded SWAP_OPPORTUNITIES regexes.

Basis
direct: src/services/PantryTaxonomyService.js:693,704,719, src/services/RecipeEnhancementSwaps.js:10-101,162, src/services/RecipeEnhancementNutrition.js:182-224, docs/solutions/logic-errors/asymmetric-calorie-deficit-tapering-and-taxonomy-elevation.md.
Rationale
Every new Tesco product added to Pantry becomes combinatorially usable in both directions with zero regex maintenance or broken unit-weight swaps.
Downsides
Pantry items bound only at categoryId level still need role/form tagging to avoid edge-case mismatches.

6. Plateau-Aware Lexicographic Tie-Breaking & Churn Gate

Search & Scoring Algorithm Confidence: 90% Complexity: Small

Because fitScoreWeights.js grants 100 across wide plateaus, rank equal or near-equal combinations lexicographically: (1) official finalScore (within a +3 pt churn gate over baseline isAutoDefault picks), (2) min(fitScoreE, fitScoreC) fairness so one partner never scores red behind a green average, (3) autoDefaultStrategy (compareProductsByStrategy), and (4) lowest basket cost.

Basis
direct: src/utils/fitScoreCalculator.js:182-183, src/utils/fitScoreWeights.js:14-29,109-120, src/services/PantryHierarchyService.js:65-104, src/services/ShoppingCalculationService.js:50-91.
Rationale
Prevents noisy product swaps for +1 point gains while turning wide Fit Score plateaus into deterministic £ savings and Elliott/Chloe balance.
Downsides
Requires choosing a sensible churn threshold (e.g. +3 pts) so small wins aren't over-suppressed.

7. Two-Stage Auto-Enhance & "Lock One, Re-Solve Rest" Attribution

Evaluation Triggers, Caching & UX Surfaces Confidence: 85% Complexity: Medium

Run the pantry combo solver as Stage A inside RecipeEnhancementService and RecipeDetailModal before proposing quantity/serving edits or creating duplicate .enhanced variants. Badge only the 1–2 non-default lines inline (e.g. 5% → 12% Mince (+11 Fit)), and when a user pins or swaps one product in RecipeDetailModal or ProductSwapModal, re-solve the remaining unlocked lines in <2 ms to compensate.

Basis
direct: src/services/RecipeEnhancementService.js:440-545, src/components/recipe/RecipeDetailModalUI.js:66-106, src/components/ProductSwapModal.js:77-139,274-317.
Rationale
Captures Fit Score gains on original recipes without cloning variants, while making automatic product picks transparent and reversible.
Downsides
Adds small UI badge/pin affordances to RecipeDetailModalUI that must stay compact on mobile.
